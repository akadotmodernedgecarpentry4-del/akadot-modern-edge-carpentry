Annual Report 2026: January - November 2026

1. Message From Chairman

In 2026, we started with nothing but hand tools and 18 determined youth in Akadot. No workshop, no machines, no donor. Today, we are a registered group, with a bank account, a website, and products in churches and homes in Kadami. We donated furniture to Kabura PAG Church, made beds, and trained youth - all by self-help. This report shows what we achieved with our own hands.

2. Who We Are

Founded Feb 2025 in Akadot Village. Registered 29 May 2026 at Kadami Sub-County - KD/19/05/26/087. Members: 18 youth (12 male, 6 female) aged 18-379. Meetings: Executive every 1st Saturday in Akadot Village. Akadot has ~850 people, ~210 idle youth. There was 0 carpentry workshop in Akadot before us.

3. What We Achieved in 2026

  • Organisation: Registered KD/19/05/26/087, Opened Bank Account Finance Trust Bank 307214002162, Created Email + Facebook Page + Website
  • Training: Trained 12 youth in basic carpentry with hand tools - measuring, cutting, joining, finishing, making beds, tables, pews, atabo
  • Production - Community Service: Donated 2 Tables + 4 Church Pews (Each Pew is 6-Seater Bench with Backrest) to Kabura PAG Church, Kadami - FREE kind donation. Total 24 seats for church members.
  • Production - Sales: Made and sold 12 beds (5x6 beds 400k - 650k) in Kadami. Carved mortar / atabo. Made samples: kitchen organizer (400x340mm), dish rack (380x300mm), desk organizer (16cm)
  • Marketing: Started professional invoice system on website with Akadot logo and bank details

5. Challenges

1. No Proper Workshop - we work in open space, rain stops work. 2. No havey duty Machines - cutting by hand is slow, 1 pew takes many days. 3. No Capital - cannot buy timber in bulk. 4. Still Sub-County Group - cannot yet bid for big District tenders.
