About Akadot Modern Edge Carpentry Group

Akadot is a youth-led Community Based Organisation in Kumi, Eastern Uganda. We train youth in carpentry and entrepreneurship to fight unemployment.

Our 5-Year Plan 2026-2030

• Train 100+ youth in carpentry & entrepreneurship
• Expand to 30 communities across Kumi District
• Equip workshop with modern tools & safety gear
• Produce 500+ furniture items for schools & clinics
