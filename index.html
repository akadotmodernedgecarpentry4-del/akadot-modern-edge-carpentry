🌍 Who We Are

Akadot Modern Edge Carpentry is a Community Based Organization (CBO) based in Akadot Trading Center, Kadami Parish, Kumi District. Started by Papa Ekalamu in 1980s as small workshop, now registered CBO empowering youth, women, school dropouts through carpentry skills.

Location: Along Kumi-Ngora Road, Akadot Trading Center
Workshop: Open Mon-Sat 8am-6pm
Contact: modernedgecarpentry4@gmail.com

👁️ Vision, Mission & Values

Vision: A skilled, self-reliant youth in Teso creating quality furniture for Uganda.
Mission: To train vulnerable youth in modern and traditional carpentry, produce affordable quality furniture, and preserve Iteso wood curving culture.
Core Values: Hard Work, Honesty, Quality, Community, Respect for Elders.

🪘 4. Cultural Preservation – Papa Ekalamu Legacy

Papa Ekalamu (Founder, 68 yrs) keeps Iteso traditional craft alive: Drums (Ajem), Mortars (Atabo), Stools, Bows. He trains youth to curve wood by hand. Custom cultural orders welcome. This is our identity.
