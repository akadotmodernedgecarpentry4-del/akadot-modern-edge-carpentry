WHAT WE NEED & WHY

📋 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We are Akadot Modern Edge Carpentry, a youth-led group in Akadot Village, Akadot Parish, Kadami Sub-County, Kumi District (Reg No: amKD/19/05/26/087). Founded by OKIRIA ASHA MORRIS and led by our Master Instructor Ariaitum MICHAEL (Muze) with 40+ years experience.

We currently train vulnerable youth using borrowed and less efficient hand tools, in a small rented space. We seek support from any prospective donor to expand into a WELL-ESTABLISHED WORKSHOP FOR YOUTH SKILLS TRAINING AND INNOVATION CENTRE to be called AKADOT COMMUNITY YOUTH SKILLS DEVELOPMENT CENTRE.

This will be our own community-based centre on 1 acre of customary land donated by one of the elders in Akadot Parish, training 50 youth annually in modern carpentry, welding, tailoring, entrepreneurship and digital innovation. It will improve family (household) sustainable livelihoods and contribute to Parish Development Model (PDM) and Vision 2040 of Uganda.

🌍 BACKGROUND & PROBLEM

Kadami Sub-County has no functional vocational Training centre. About 70-80% of youth are unemployed, school drop-outs due to poverty, orphanhood, and early pregnancy.

The nearest training centre is in Kumi Town, about 25km away from Akadot, making it very expensive in transport costs, leading poor families not to afford hostel and boarding fees. This has made many families stay back at home without advancing in any skills training.

Our Proof: From Feb to Nov 2026, we trained 25 youth informally in our small workshop. 8 now earn 30k-50k UGX per day by selling products made by them. Having seen this improvement, demand is now overwhelming – about 70 youth are on waiting list.

But we operate on small rented land that cannot accommodate bigger number of learners. We have therefore secured commitment for 1 acre of customary land in Akadot Parish from one of the elders, to build our own centre. We only need support to develop it into a well-established Training and Innovative Workshop in Kadami Sub-County, Kumi District.
